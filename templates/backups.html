Every ZVM writes a full export of its VPGs' settings once a day. The monitor stores each new one within the hour - every VPG's RPO, journal, recovery target, networks, VMs, volumes, NICs, boot order and scripts - compressed and encrypted, and keeps them {{ days }} days, off the ZVM. Engineers can open any backup and compare it with the one before; an admin can re-create a VPG that has been deleted. Reading the exports changes nothing on the ZVM.
